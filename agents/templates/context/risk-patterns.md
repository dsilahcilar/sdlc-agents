# Risk Patterns

Recurring technical and delivery risks to consider during planning, coding, and review.

---

## How to Use

- Planning Agent: include relevant risks in feature/task context.
- Architect Agent: evaluate structural and debt risk exposure.
- Code Review Agent: flag matching risk signals in changes.
- Retro Agent: add new patterns after incidents.

---

## Risk Categories

| Category | Prefix | Focus |
|----------|--------|-------|
| Structural | `STRUCT-` | Architecture and dependency risks |
| Process | `PROC-` | Workflow and execution risks |
| Integration | `INTEG-` | External service/infrastructure risks |
| Data | `DATA-` | Data integrity and migration risks |
| Security | `SEC-` | Security and compliance risks |

---

## Pattern Template

```markdown
### <CATEGORY>-NNN: <Risk Name>

**Description:** <What the risk is>

**Signals:**
- <Signal 1>
- <Signal 2>

**Why it's risky:**
- <Potential impact>

**Mitigation:**
- <Preventive or corrective action>
```
