## Brief

**Goal.** One or two sentences: what is true for the user when this is done.

**Not in scope.** What this change leaves alone on purpose.

**Acceptance checks.** Commands or steps that come back pass or fail.

- [ ] `<command or step>`

**Blast radius.** Delete the lines that do not apply.

- Stored data: what shape changes, and how to go back.
- API or contract: who reads it, and whether the readers that are live today survive the new answer.
- Release order: what must ship first.
- Other repositories or services.

**Lane.** small, standard or heavy.
